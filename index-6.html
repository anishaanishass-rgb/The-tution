YOUR STUDY BOOST

Make study time work for you 🚀

You don't have to learn everything at once. A simple routine can help you understand and remember more.

  • ✓Study in short, focused sessions.
  • ✓Explain a new idea in your own words.
  • ✓Take breaks and get enough sleep.

Small steps, big progress 🌱

Try this mini learning plan whenever you sit down to study.

  • 1Review: Remember what you learned last time.
  • 2Explore: Learn one new concept.
  • 3Practice: Answer a question without looking at your notes.
  • 4Reflect: Notice what you understand and what to revisit.
